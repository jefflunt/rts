# Design and implement an interactive, cached 256x256 floating minimap overlay in the bottom-left corner of the client viewport. The minimap scales to support 64x64, 128x128, and 256x256 maps, caches tile-type color dots on an offscreen image buffer, displays a double-lined metallic border, overlays a white rectangle representing the camera viewport, and allows clicking or dragging on the minimap to instantly center and clamp the camera viewport to that world position.

This task involves designing and implementing a 256x256 pixel floating minimap overlay positioned at the bottom-left corner of the Ebitengine client viewport. The minimap will display the entire tilemap, cleanly scaling based on map dimensions (64x64, 128x128, and 256x256). To optimize performance, the background is drawn once to a cached image buffer where tiles are rendered as colored dots based on their TileType. It will feature a 2-pixel double-lined metallic border and display a bright white rectangular overlay representing the current camera viewport. Crucially, the minimap must handle mouse clicks and drags to instantly translate minimap screen coordinates back to world pixels, centering and clamping the camera viewport at that world position.
